A LITTLE PLANET NEEDS YOU

Hold the line.

Block comets with your glowing shield. Use ← → or drag around the planet.

Ready when you are.

Hold the curved-arrow buttons on touchscreens, use the keyboard arrows, or drag the shield directly. P pauses. R restarts.

Only one world to save.

Incoming comets fly toward your planet. Sweep the mint-green arc around its orbit to catch them before impact.

Scoring

  • Block a comet: earn 10 points, with growing combo bonuses.
  • Miss: your planet loses a heart, and your combo resets.
  • Golden comets: repair one heart when caught.
  • The longer you survive, the faster the sky gets.
No servers. Your personal best is stored locally in this browser when storage is available. You can play without saving anything online.
