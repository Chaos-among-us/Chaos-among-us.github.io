Small shop. Real decisions.

You have a limited number of days to reach the cash target. Each morning choose exactly one move. Then open the shop and sell as much as your shelves and demand allow.

Trading rules

  • Each item sells for the displayed price.
  • Daily rent is deducted even when you sell nothing.
  • Restocking costs money but provides inventory.
  • Some events affect shoppers or charge an unavoidable bill.
  • Finish with cash at or above the target. Unsold stock isn't counted as cash.
Forecasts and events are fixed for each scenario, so you can try a different strategy after replaying. No luck-based surprise costs.
