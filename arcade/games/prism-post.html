First delivery
Tap a glowing mirror to rotate it.

Mirrors are buttons: Tab to select, Enter or Space to turn. The bright green line shows where the beam travels.

Mail, but make it light.

The postmaster sends one beam in from the board's edge. Deliver it to the blue crystal by turning the golden mirrors.

How to play

  • Tap or click a golden mirror to switch between / and \.
  • The ray turns ninety degrees each time it touches a mirror.
  • Bright crystal means the mail arrived. Then choose the next stage.
  • Use Undo or Restart if the delivery gets lost.
Designer note. These are hand-authored routes with extra decoy mirrors. There's no countdown; try different paths at your own pace.
